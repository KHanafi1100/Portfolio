Implementation

Processed a 5% subset of the AG News training split and divided articles into 300-character chunks with 50-character overlap. Generated sentence embeddings with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and saved the vector index using FAISS.

Integrated a quantized Mistral 7B Instruct model through LangChain and CTransformers to generate responses from retrieved context.

Experiments

Compared default similarity retrieval, maximum marginal relevance (MMR), embedding-based contextual filtering, a custom prompt, and an expanded retrieval set.

Recorded example responses and inspected how retrieval and prompting choices affected the generated answers.

Evaluation

Evaluation consists of qualitative comparisons of saved responses. Article and chunk counts are recorded in the notebook outputs.

Planned improvements

Display source passages alongside generated answers, add systematic grounding evaluation, and provide reproducible setup instructions.

Project status

Academic prototype using a historical dataset. Live inference is unavailable on this site.
